🎃 Grimmwick — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

The short version: Grimmwick collects nothing. No analytics, no ads, no tracking, no accounts, and no personal information — from anyone, of any age.

What the app stores

Your game progress (levels beaten, candy, costumes, settings) is saved only on your device, using the device's local storage. It never leaves your device, and we cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes the save.

What the app does NOT do

  • No data collection of any kind ("Data Not Collected" on the App Store label)
  • No third-party analytics or advertising SDKs
  • No account creation or sign-in
  • No network connection required — the game is fully playable offline
  • No purchases in this version

Children's privacy

Grimmwick is rated 4+. Because the app collects no data at all, there is no personal information from children (or anyone else) to collect, share, or sell.

Changes

If a future update ever changes any of the above (for example, optional Game Center leaderboards), this policy will be updated first and the App Store privacy label will reflect it.
