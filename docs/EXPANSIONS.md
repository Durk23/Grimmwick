# Grimmwick — Expansion plans (owner vision, captured Aug 2026)

Two paid add-on expansions after the free v1.0 launch. Each is base-game-sized
(25 levels, district structure) and lands IN ITS SEASON — every drop is a fresh
App Store featuring opportunity timed to when players want that world.

## Expansion 1: WINTERFEST (target: December)
**The canon on-ramp is already shipped**: the v1.0 finale ends with the first
snow falling over the festival ("...is that snow?"). The base game literally
promises this expansion in its ending.

- Story seed: Grimmwick's first WINTERFEST — the town celebrates a full year of
  the relit night. But the deep cold has its own forgotten spirits... Grimm, now
  night-watchman, is on YOUR side this time (hint-giver NPC, maybe playable).
- The new verb (every era gets one; base game's was CLIMB): **SLIDE/SKATE** —
  ice physics, momentum lines, frozen rivers as speed-run lanes.
- Relight theme continues: ice melts near lit lanterns — light literally opens
  paths. Frozen-over versions of beloved places + new frost districts.
- Roster seeds: snowball-rolling critters (grow as they roll), scarf-wearing
  snow-boos, a mini-boss snowman who keeps rebuilding himself.

## Expansion 2: THE SUNKEN ISLES (beach/jungle/water — target: summer)
**Canon bridge shipped too**: beating Captain Wraith brought the SEA back to
Ghost Harbor. With the ocean returned, ships can sail again — the Salty
Phantom (friendly now) carries Pip to the isles.

- The new verb: **SWIM** (+ sail between level clusters?). Water levels breathe
  differently — buoyancy, currents, dive pockets. Tide-platform + cannon-launch
  mechanics from D4 reuse directly.
- Jungle: vine-climb reuse, giant flowers as bounce-pads, waterfalls.
- Roster seeds: coconut-lobbing monkeys-in-ghost-sheets, a pufferfish that
  puffs when stared at (the Boo rule inverted), Peg-Leg Polly returns as guide.

## Business shape (decided direction, refine at launch +30 days)
- Base game: free forever, no ads, candy economy only.
- Each expansion: one **non-consumable IAP** (price TBD vs comps; $4.99–6.99
  territory). First level of each expansion playable FREE as the hook —
  try-before-you-buy converts far better than a locked door.
- Restore Purchases button required. StoreKit 2 direct (no account system).
- Nightmare Mode (remix of the 25 base levels) stays a FREE update between
  expansions — free content keeps ratings/featuring healthy while paid
  expansions carry revenue.

## Production reality
The whole pipeline exists: district kits, enemy contract, gamble containers,
map screen, difficulty tuning table, agent-fleet level builds + adversarial
review + the playability gauntlet (height audit, climb audit, spawn-safety,
pit dressing). Each base-game district took roughly a day of pipeline time.
A 25-level expansion is a few focused weeks including polish — the long poles
are DESIGN taste passes and seasonal audio, not construction.

Rule that carries over: every expansion level obeys the constitution
(determinism, hearts-always, one gimmick, comparable heights, telegraphs).
